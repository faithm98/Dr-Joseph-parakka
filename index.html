Our Services & Initiatives - 19 Points

  1. General Clinic
  2. Child Neurodevelopment Clinic for prevention/minimisation of Lifelong handicaps.
  3. When Neonatologists and Pediatricians invite, then baby-friendly Neurodevelopmental procedures to be started in NICUs, PICUs, & Pediatric Wards.
  4. Baby-friendly Neurodevelopmental assessment of all newborns either on the day of discharge from the hospital or the earliest day after that, followed by monthly follow-ups ( takes only few minutes).
  5. To start early baby-friendly Neurodevelopmental procedures whenever the Child is heading for Lifelong handicaps ( Neurodevelopmental disorders).
  6. Even in Chromosomal disorders like Down Syndrome, baby-friendly Neurodevelopmental procedures from newborn period will definitely elevate the IQ & EQ of the Child.
  7. Baby-friendly Neurodevelopmental procedures for all full-blown Neurodevelopmental Disorders.
  8. Montessori House of Children.
  9. Model International Integrated School where each Class will have a maximum of 24 Students, where-in a maximum of 6 differently abled Students are present( along with Special Educators in each Neurodevelopmental constructs). Emphasis not only on academics but also on physical fitness exercises, swimming, self-defense techniques, Scouts/NCC, debates, extempore Speech, music, dancing, Sports & games, encouraging creative hobbies, teaching entrepreneurship, etc.
  10. Individualised education programs for differently abled students.
  11. A doctor trained in Child Neurodevelopment & Rehabilitation will be present during every Parent-teacher meeting.
  12. Pubertal & Pre-pubertal sex-education of children in the presence of their parents.
  13. CBT (Cognitive Behavior Therapy) & IC (Integrative Counseling) for persons with personality problems, unhealthy addictions , alcoholism, etc.
  14. Pre-marital, Marital, and Pre-pregnancy counseling; including natural family planning methods like Billing's Ovulation Method.
  15. Pregnancy-period counseling if the Obstetrician requests.
  16. Educating the Public on health matters through various means. Health is not just the absence of disease, but total physical, mental, social, psychological, and financial well-being.
  17. Organising free Medical Camps off & on
  18. Home consultation & Home counselling
  19. On-line consultation & On-line Counselling
