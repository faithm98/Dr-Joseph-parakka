Our Mission & Services

Health is not just absence of disease, but total physical, mental, social, psychological, and financial well-being. Focus on early intervention to prevent lifelong handicaps.

Child Neurodevelopment Clinic

  • Baby friendly newborn assessment on the day of discharge or earliest day after that (which takes only few mins)
  • Monthly follow ups
  • Early procedures in NICUs, PICUs & Pediatric Wards
  • Support for Down Syndrome & chromosomal disorders to elevate IQ & EQ
  • Care for all full-blown Neurodevelopmental Disorders

Integrated Education

  • Model International Integrated School: Max 24 students per class
  • Maximum 6 differently abled students in the class with Special Educators
  • Montessori House of Children
  • Individualised education programs
  • Emphasis: academics, sports, music, swimming, self-defense, NCC, debates
  • Doctor present during every Parent-Teacher meeting

Counseling & Therapy

  • CBT and Integrated Counseling - Poor scholastic performance
  • CBT & Integrative Counseling for personality problems, addictions
  • Pre-marital, Marital & Pre-pregnancy counseling
  • Natural family planning – Billing's Ovulation Method
  • Pubertal and prepubertal sex education in the presence of parents
  • Pregnancy-period counseling if Obstetrician requests

Community Outreach

  • Medical College cum University Project
  • General Clinic services
  • Organising free Medical Camps
  • Home consultation & Home counselling
  • Online consultation & Online counselling
